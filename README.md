<p align="center"><b>H E I M D A L L</b></p>
<hr style="color:#808080;">

Heimdall learns a generative model of the ocean state in a latent space, conditioned on surface observations of the ocean made from space. Its goal is to generate very long joint trajectories of the ocean state and of the satellite observations, in order to determine the limits of the predictability of the ocean from space.
